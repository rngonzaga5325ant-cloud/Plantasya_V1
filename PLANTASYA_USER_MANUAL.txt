DOCUMENT OVERVIEW & WELCOME PREFACE
================================================================================
Welcome to the official Plantasya User Manual. This document serves as a complete,
accessible, non-technical operational manual for the Plantasya mobile application.

Whether you are caring for your very first indoor houseplant or maintaining a
diverse collection of tropical plants, Plantasya simplifies plant identification,
care task tracking, reminder scheduling, and botanical learning.

No previous technical or IT experience is needed to read and follow this manual.
Every chapter includes plain-language descriptions, clear layout diagrams, tabular
guides, and step-by-step procedures.

CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION TO PLANTASYA
================================================================================
1.1 Application Purpose & Core Mission:
Plantasya is an intelligent mobile plant care management system designed for
Android smartphones. The application serves three key functions:
1. Instant AI Plant Recognition: Identifies plant species in under a second using
   your phone's camera and built-in artificial intelligence.
2. Automated Plant Care Scheduling: Calculates tailored watering, fertilizing,
   leaf dusting, and soil check schedules based on plant species.
3. Interactive Botanical Education: Provides comprehensive care guidelines, pet
   toxicity advisories, height ranges, space footprint requirements, and
   interactive 3D models.

1.2 Target Audience & User Profiles:
* Homeowners & Apartment Dwellers: Keep indoor house plants healthy without
  forgetting care routines.
* Pet Owners: Quickly verify if a house plant is safe or toxic to cats and dogs.
* Beginner Gardeners: Access clear, simple guidelines on light, humidity,
  temperature, and soil needs.
* Plant Collectors: Build a digital catalog of owned plants and maintain an
  activity log of all scanned plants.

1.3 Key Features Summary:
* AI Camera Scanner: On-device TensorFlow Lite classification for offline plant
  identification.
* Personal Dashboard: Carousel display showing all owned plants and active daily
  care tasks.
* Task Generator & Alarm System: Automatically schedules care tasks and triggers
  exact Android alarm notifications.
* Botanical Encyclopedia: Searchable catalog with care specifications for
  pre-loaded plant species.
* Interactive 3D Model Viewer: Real-time 3D plant models with 360-degree touch
  rotation, zoom, and panning.
* Scan History Log: Stores photos, dates, times, and species names for every
  scanned plant.

CHAPTER 2: SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS & DEVICE PERMISSIONS
================================================================================
2.1 Hardware and Software Requirements:
* Operating System: Android 8.0 (Oreo / API Level 26) or higher.
* Camera: Rear camera with autofocus capability.
* Internal Storage: Minimum 150 MB free space.
* Screen Display: HD Resolution (720p or higher) recommended.

2.2 Permissions Guide:
- CAMERA: Requested on opening AI Scanner. Required to capture live photos.
- READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Requested in Profile Setup.
  Required to load custom avatars from photo gallery.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Requested on first launch. Displays push
  notification care alerts.
- SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM (Android 12+): System prompt. Triggers precise care alarms.

2.3 Granting Permissions Step-by-Step:
1. Notifications: Tap "Allow" when prompted on first launch.
2. Exact Alarms: On Android 12+, tap "Settings" on the prompt dialog, enable the
   switch for Plantasya, and return.
3. Camera: Tap "While using the app" when opening scanner for the first time.

CHAPTER 3: ACCOUNT CREATION, LOGIN & SESSION MANAGEMENT
================================================================================
3.1 Application Splash Screen & Launch:
When Plantasya is opened, a dark splash screen displays the pulsing logo for
3 seconds while initializing local databases. Logged-in users are navigated
directly to the Dashboard.

3.2 Landing Screen Navigation:
New or logged-out users can select either LOG IN or SIGN UP.

3.3 Registering a New Account:
1. Tap SIGN UP on Landing Screen.
2. Enter a unique Username.
3. Enter Password and Confirm Password.
4. Tap SIGN UP to proceed to Profile Setup.

3.4 Profile Setup:
1. Tap circular avatar frame to choose a photo from gallery.
2. Enter Display Name (e.g., "Maria Gardener").
3. Tap SAVE PROFILE to complete registration.

3.5 Logging In:
1. Tap LOG IN on Landing Screen.
2. Enter Username and Password.
3. Tap LOG IN.

3.6 Session Persistence:
Your session remains saved across app restarts and phone reboots until you select
Log Out.

3.7 Changing Credentials:
Go to Profile > Settings > Change Credentials. Enter current credentials and new
desired credentials, then tap SAVE CHANGES.

3.8 Logging Out:
Go to Profile > Settings > Log Out. Tap YES on confirmation.

3.9 Account Deletion:
Go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. Tap DELETE on the warning prompt.
All profile data, saved plants, history, and active alarms are permanently erased.

CHAPTER 4: MAIN DASHBOARD & DAILY PLANT CARE SCHEDULING
================================================================================
4.1 Dashboard Layout:
- Top: OWNED PLANTS carousel header and avatar.
- Center: 3D animated visual carousel showing cards for owned plants.
- Bottom: Dynamic daily care tasks list corresponding to selected plant.
- Action: Green + ADD PLANT button.

4.2 Carousel Navigation:
Swipe left or right across the top carousel to view different plants. The task list
below updates instantly to show tasks for the active plant.

4.3 Empty State:
If no plants are owned, tap + ADD PLANT and choose SCAN PLANT or CHOOSE FROM
LIBRARY.

4.4 Automated Task Generation:
Adding a plant creates automatic species-specific care schedules (e.g.,
Watering, Fertilizing, Leaf Cleaning).

4.5 Completing Tasks:
Tap the checkbox on a task card to mark it as done for the day.

4.6 Custom Reminders:
Tap the Clock Icon next to any task. Select Date and Time to schedule a custom
alarm.

4.7 Android Alarm Notifications:
Plantasya registers exact system alarms. Alarms ring even when the app is closed or
the screen is off. Reboots automatically restore all scheduled reminders.

CHAPTER 5: AI PLANT IDENTIFICATION & CAMERA SCANNING
================================================================================
5.1 On-Device TensorFlow Lite Model:
Runs offline machine learning inference directly on device with a 75% minimum
confidence filter.

5.2 Opening Scanner:
Tap + ADD PLANT > SCAN PLANT or tap floating camera button in History tab.

5.3 Framing & Lighting:
Ensure clear natural indoor lighting. Hold camera 1 to 2 feet away from plant leaves.

5.4 Capture Process:
Align leaves in center frame overlay and tap the circular Capture Button.

5.5 Scan Results:
Select "YES, I OWN THIS PLANT" to save to dashboard and schedule care tasks, or
"NO, JUST SEARCHING" to log in history only.

5.6 Low Confidence Scans:
If confidence is below 75%, result shows "Unknown Plant" with a "TRY SCANNING AGAIN"
button.

CHAPTER 10: STEP-BY-STEP OPERATING PROCEDURES (10 WORKFLOWS)
================================================================================
WORKFLOW 1: Registration & Setup
1. Open App > Tap SIGN UP.
2. Enter Username and Password > Tap SIGN UP.
3. Select Avatar photo > Enter Display Name > Tap SAVE PROFILE.

WORKFLOW 2: Scanning a Plant
1. Tap Dashboard > + ADD PLANT > SCAN PLANT.
2. Frame plant in daylight > Tap Capture Button.
3. Tap "YES, I OWN THIS PLANT".

WORKFLOW 3: Adding Plant from Library
1. Open Library > Search plant name.
2. Tap plant card > Tap Hollow Heart Icon.

WORKFLOW 4: Setting Care Alarms
1. Open Dashboard > Select plant card.
2. Tap Clock Icon next to task > Pick Date and Time > Tap OK.

WORKFLOW 5: 3D Model Inspection
1. Open Library > Select plant > Tap VIEW INTERACTIVE 3D MODEL.
2. Tap "GOT IT!" > Drag single finger to rotate in 3D.

WORKFLOW 6: Converting Scans in History
1. Tap History tab > Locate scan item > Tap Hollow Heart Icon.

WORKFLOW 7: Changing Credentials
1. Tap Profile > Settings > Change Credentials.
2. Enter current credentials, enter new credentials > Tap SAVE CHANGES.

WORKFLOW 8: Removing an Owned Plant
1. Open Library > Select owned plant > Tap Filled Heart Icon > Tap REMOVE.

WORKFLOW 9: Inspecting Pet Toxicity
1. Open Library > Search plant > Open details > Check Toxicity line item.

WORKFLOW 10: Deleting Account
1. Tap Profile > Settings > Delete Account > Tap DELETE.

CHAPTER 11: COMPLETE BOTANICAL CATALOG REFERENCE GUIDE
================================================================================
1. AGLAONEMA (Aglaonema commutatum)
   Description: Glossy patterned leaves; low-maintenance.
   Light: Low to bright indirect. Water: Allow top 1-2 inches to dry.
   Humidity: Moderate. Temp: 65-80 F. Soil: Well-draining with perlite.
   Fertilizer: Monthly. Toxicity: Toxic to pets. Height: 1-3 ft. Compact footprint.

2. POTHOS (Epipremnum aureum)
   Description: Fast-growing trailing vine with heart-shaped leaves.
   Light: Low to bright indirect. Water: Allow top 2 inches to dry.
   Humidity: Average. Temp: 65-85 F. Soil: All-purpose potting mix.
   Fertilizer: Monthly. Toxicity: Toxic to pets. Height: Trailing 6-10 ft.

3. CALATHEA (Calathea spp.)
   Description: Patterned foliage with daily leaf movement.
   Light: Low to medium indirect. Water: Keep evenly moist.
   Humidity: High. Temp: 65-80 F. Soil: Organic rich, slightly acidic.
   Fertilizer: Monthly. Toxicity: Non-toxic to pets. Height: 1-2 ft.

4. MONEY TREE (Pachira aquatica)
   Description: Braided trunk tree symbolizing good luck.
   Light: Bright indirect. Water: Water when top 2-3 inches dry.
   Humidity: Moderate. Temp: 65-80 F. Soil: Well-draining moisture retentive.
   Fertilizer: Every 4-6 weeks. Toxicity: Non-toxic to pets. Height: 3-6 ft.

5. ORCHID (Phalaenopsis)
   Description: Epiphytic orchid with long-lasting showy blooms.
   Light: Bright indirect. Water: Every 7-10 days.
   Humidity: 50-70%. Temp: 60-80 F. Soil: Chunky orchid bark mix.
   Fertilizer: Every 2-4 weeks. Toxicity: Non-toxic to pets. Height: 8-24 in.

6. PARLOR PALM (Chamaedorea elegans)
   Description: Graceful slow-growing palm tree.
   Light: Low to medium indirect. Water: Keep evenly moist.
   Humidity: Moderate. Temp: 65-80 F. Soil: Well-draining organic rich.
   Fertilizer: Monthly. Toxicity: Non-toxic to pets. Height: 2-6 ft.

7. ZZ PLANT (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)
   Description: Glossy upright stems; highly durable.
   Light: Low to bright indirect. Water: Drought tolerant; dry completely.
   Humidity: Average. Temp: 65-85 F. Soil: Cactus/succulent mix.
   Fertilizer: Every 6-8 weeks. Toxicity: Toxic if ingested. Height: 1-3 ft.

8. PEACE LILY (Spathiphyllum spp.)
   Description: Deep green foliage with white spathe blooms.
   Light: Low to bright indirect. Water: Keep lightly moist.
   Humidity: Moderate to high. Temp: 65-85 F. Soil: Rich well-draining soil.
   Fertilizer: 3 times per year. Toxicity: Mildly toxic to pets. Height: 1-4 ft.

9. SNAKE PLANT (Dracaena trifasciata)
   Description: Architectural vertical sword-like leaves.
   Light: Low light to direct sun. Water: Allow to dry completely.
   Humidity: Low to moderate. Temp: 60-85 F. Soil: Coarse succulent mix.
   Fertilizer: Monthly. Toxicity: Toxic to pets. Height: 1-4 ft.

10. DRACAENA (Dracaena spp.)
    Description: Tropical tree with strap-like foliage on woody canes.
    Light: Medium to bright indirect. Water: Allow top 1-2 inches to dry.
    Humidity: Moderate. Temp: 65-80 F. Soil: Well-draining organic rich.
    Fertilizer: Monthly. Toxicity: Toxic to pets. Height: 2-10 ft.

CHAPTER 12: TROUBLESHOOTING, FAQS & MAINTENANCE
================================================================================
Troubleshooting:
- AI Camera says "Unknown Plant": Move 1-2 ft closer under bright lighting.
- Reminders not ringing: Enable Notifications and Exact Alarms in phone settings.
- Avatar upload fails: Select PNG or JPEG image under 10 MB in size.

FAQs:
1. Is internet required? No, Plantasya operates completely offline.
2. Will restarting my phone erase data? No, all data is saved in Room database.
3. Is Plantasya pet safe? Yes, all plants feature pet toxicity warnings.
